# Safety Spec

## Principle

Safety beats performance. Every engine module can raise risk flags. Hard stops stop automatic plans and require qualified support.

## Hard Stops

Automatic plans stop when any of these are active:

- fainting
- confusion
- chest pain
- severe headache
- severe cramping
- persistent vomiting
- inability to urinate
- very dark urine plus dehydration symptoms
- acute illness
- concussion symptoms
- severe dizziness
- eating-disorder risk signals
- rapid unexplained weight loss
- medical contraindication
- pregnancy or possible pregnancy when a cut/training decision could be unsafe
- very heavy bleeding with dizziness/faintness
- unusual pelvic pain
- missed period plus under-fueling signs

## Risk Severity

- `info`: context the athlete should know.
- `caution`: plan may continue with safer defaults.
- `high`: plan is restricted and may require review.
- `critical`: automatic plan blocked.

## Professional Review Triggers

- acute weight manipulation above internal caps
- same-day weigh-in with meaningful acute loss
- repeated missed nutrition targets plus rapid weight loss
- RED-S/under-fueling indicators
- ED risk
- medical history conflicts
- hypertension/kidney/cardiac flags for fluid/electrolyte decisions
- severe cycle symptoms
- concussion symptoms
- minor athlete weight-cut request

## Minor Athlete Rules

Minors cannot receive acute weight manipulation protocols. The engine may show education, stable fueling, coach/guardian/professional review prompts, and safer class discussion.

## Under-Fueling Screening

Inputs:

- rapid loss
- missed targets
- low energy availability proxy
- fatigue
- recurrent illness
- sleep disruption
- mood changes
- menstrual disruption when relevant
- high training load plus low intake
- restrictive eating flags

Outputs:

- caution/high/critical risk flag
- calorie deficit blocked or reduced
- recovery nutrition protected
- review prompt

## Dehydration Risk

Inputs:

- water logs
- electrolyte logs
- urine color
- dizziness
- fainting
- dark urine
- heat exposure
- sweat-heavy sessions
- weight change
- sodium restriction
- excessive plain water

Actions:

- block unsafe cut
- warn about plain-water overdrinking
- add electrolyte guidance
- seek medical support for hard-stop symptoms

## Cycle-Related Safety

Flags:

- very heavy bleeding
- dizziness/faintness
- unusual pelvic pain
- migraine with concerning symptoms
- missed period plus under-fueling signs
- possible pregnancy
- aggressive cut during high symptom burden

Cycle data remains private and consent-based.

## Unsafe Cut Handling

When blocked, the engine shows:

- automatic plan stopped
- reason and evidence
- safer alternatives
- professional-review requirement
- no hidden instructions for dehydration methods

Alternatives:

- move weight class
- extend timeline
- stop cut
- maintain training quality
- seek coach/dietitian/medical review
