# Boxing Training Spec

## Responsibility

The training engine generates boxing-specific S&C and performance support around protected boxing anchors. It never prescribes live sparring, unsupervised fight simulation, or contact coaching.

## Protected Anchors

Protected anchors are athlete or coach-owned sessions:

- boxing class
- technical session
- pads/mitts
- bag work
- footwork session
- sparring
- roadwork
- coach-assigned strength
- competition
- travel
- recovery day

Generated sessions adapt around anchors. They do not move or delete anchors.

## Boxing Levels

Supported tracks:

- aspiring_boxer
- amateur_novice
- amateur_open
- amateur_elite
- pro_development
- pro_4_6_round
- pro_8_10_round
- pro_12_round

Beginners receive lower complexity, lower variance, and technical basics. Advanced and pro athletes receive more precise power, conditioning, and taper logic.

## Rules

- Hard generated work cannot stack on sparring or competition days.
- High-intensity intervals stay away from hard sparring unless explicitly justified.
- Strength/power must not impair key boxing sessions.
- Taper reduces volume and preserves speed/power quality.
- Camp emphasizes specificity, recovery, and preserving boxing performance.
- Build phase can emphasize broader athletic development.
- Skill-support microdoses remain low-risk.
- Red readiness blocks hard generated work.
- Orange readiness reduces hard generated work.
- Pain flags restrict movement patterns.
- High cycle symptom burden trims optional volume/intensity.
- Missed sessions trigger repeat/regress before novelty.

## Strength Session Template

Each strength session includes:

- warm-up
- movement prep
- main lift or focus
- secondary lift
- unilateral, posterior chain, or trunk support
- boxing-relevant accessory
- cooldown or mobility
- RPE/RIR target
- rest guidance
- tempo guidance
- progression rules
- substitutions
- safety notes
- cycle/readiness modifications
- boxing rationale

## Power Session Template

Power work is low-volume and high-intent:

- med ball throws
- jumps or bounds when eligible
- rotational transfer
- Olympic-lift derivatives only for eligible athletes
- full recovery
- stop when quality drops
- no fatigue-chasing finishers

## Conditioning And Roadwork

Conditioning distinguishes:

- Zone 2 aerobic base
- tempo/threshold support
- aerobic power intervals
- alactic repeat work
- glycolytic round tolerance
- boxing-round support
- recovery circulation

Roadwork supports HR, RPE, talk-test, and pace options. No-wearable mode uses RPE and talk-test.

## Progression

Allowed decisions:

- progress volume
- progress intensity
- repeat
- regress
- deload
- swap family
- coach review

Progress only when completion, RPE, pain, readiness, nutrition support, schedule load, and safety are acceptable.
