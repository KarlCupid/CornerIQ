# Rebrand And Design System

## Brand

Name: CornerIQ

Internal engine: Corner Engine

Subtitle candidate: Boxing prep, fuel & weight

Meaning:

- intelligence in your corner
- boxing-specific guidance
- performance and weight-class support
- calm, precise, coach-like decisions

## Product Voice

Tone:

- direct
- calm
- boxer-specific
- safety-first
- explanatory without being wordy
- never shaming

Avoid:

- generic fitness wording
- combat-sports primary identity
- AI coach overpromise
- bro-science hype
- "crush it"
- "hack your hormones"
- "make weight at all costs"
- fake medical certainty

## Copy Examples

- "Sparring owns today’s hard stress."
- "Fuel the rounds, not just the scale."
- "Scale is noisy today. Your trend still matters more than this spike."
- "Cycle symptoms are high. Keep the session, lower the cost."
- "This cut is blocked until weigh-in timing is confirmed."
- "Strength stays short today to protect tomorrow’s sparring."
- "Tournament mode keeps you near weight between bouts."
- "You do not need a wearable. A quick check-in is enough for today’s plan."

## Visual Direction

- dark boxing-corner base
- off-white wraps/canvas text
- muted red corner accent for urgent fight/cut/risk cues
- electric blue or cyan for active data/intelligence
- warm amber for caution
- restrained green for safe/ready states
- strong readable cards
- large gym-floor touch targets
- calm planning mode

Avoid:

- generic neon gym gradients
- decorative glassmorphism as identity
- noisy metric dashboards
- pink cycle branding
- tiny hidden safety warnings

## Typography

- Use system font stack for native performance.
- Headlines are compact and strong.
- Body copy stays readable under fatigue and sweat.
- No negative letter spacing.

## UI Principles

- The first card answers what matters now.
- Risk and confidence are visible.
- Raw charts are secondary.
- Every main recommendation has a "why" path.
- Touch targets are large.
- Cards are readable, not decorative.
- UI does not own engine decisions.
